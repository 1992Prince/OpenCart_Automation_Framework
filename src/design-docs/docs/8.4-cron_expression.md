# Cron Expression Notes

## Basic Format of Cron Job

Remember **5 stars/fields separated by spaces**:

```text
* * * * *
```

The format is:

```text
┌───────────── minute (0-59)
│ ┌─────────── hour (0-23)
│ │ ┌───────── day of month (1-31)
│ │ │ ┌─────── month (1-12)
│ │ │ │ ┌───── day of week (0-6)
│ │ │ │ │
* * * * *
```

- **First field** represents minute `[0-59]`
- **Second field** represents hour `[0-23]`
- **Third field** represents day/date of month `[1-31]`
- **Fourth field** represents month `[1-12]`
- **Fifth field** represents day of week `[0-6]`
  - `0` and `7` are Sunday
  - `1` is Monday
  - `2` is Tuesday
  - `3` is Wednesday
  - `4` is Thursday
  - `5` is Friday
  - `6` is Saturday

---

## Every Minute

```text
* * * * * echo 'Hello' >> /tmp/test.txt
```

The 5 stars represent **every minute of every hour of every day of every month, on every day of the week**.

---

## Every Hour at 30 Minutes Past the Hour

```text
30 * * * *
```

This means **at minute 30 of every hour, every day of every month, on every day of the week**.

Examples:

```text
12:30 AM
1:30 AM
2:30 AM
3:30 AM
...
11:30 PM
```

> **Important:** `30 * * * *` does **not** mean every 30 minutes.  
> For every 30 minutes, use `*/30 * * * *`.

---

## Hour Format

The hour can be anything from `0` to `23`.

- `0` = midnight
- `1` = 1 AM
- `12` = noon
- `13` = 1 PM
- `23` = 11 PM at night

---

## 5:30 AM Every Single Day

```text
30 5 * * *
```

This means **5:30 AM every day**.

---

## 5:30 AM on the 1st of Every Month

```text
30 5 1 * *
```

This means **5:30 AM on the 1st day of every month**.

---

## 5:30 AM on the 1st of January Only

```text
30 5 1 1 *
```

This means **5:30 AM on the 1st of January**.

---

## Day of Week

The last field is for the **day of week**.

If you want to run at a specific time on a specific day of the week, use the fifth field.

---

## 5:30 AM Every Monday

```text
30 5 * * 1
```

This means **5:30 AM on every Monday**.

---

## Midnight Every Monday

```text
0 0 * * 1
```

Here:

- First `0` = minute `0`
- Second `0` = midnight
- `1` = Monday

So this means **midnight every Monday**.

### Do not get confused with:

```text
* 0 * * 1
```

This means **every minute during the midnight hour on Monday**.

Examples:

```text
12:00 AM
12:01 AM
12:02 AM
...
12:59 AM
```

---

## Midnight on the 1st and 15th of Every Month

```text
0 0 1,15 * *
```

This means **midnight on the 1st and 15th of every month**.

---

## Every 10 Minutes

```text
*/10 * * * *
```

This means **every 10 minutes**.

Examples:

```text
12:00
12:10
12:20
12:30
12:40
12:50
1:00
...
```

### Every 5 Minutes

```text
*/5 * * * *
```

This means **every 5 minutes**.

### Every 30 Minutes

```text
*/30 * * * *
```

This means **every 30 minutes**.

---

## Every Hour from Midnight to 5 AM

```text
0 0-5 * * *
```

This means **at minute 0 of every hour from midnight through 5 AM**.

Examples:

```text
12:00 AM
1:00 AM
2:00 AM
3:00 AM
4:00 AM
5:00 AM
```

---

## Every Midnight on Weekdays

```text
0 0 * * 1-5
```

This means **midnight on every weekday, Monday through Friday**.

---

## Every 10 PM on Weekdays

```text
0 22 * * 1-5
```

This means **10:00 PM every weekday, Monday through Friday**.

---

## Every 10:30 PM on Weekdays

```text
30 22 * * 1-5
```

This means **10:30 PM every weekday, Monday through Friday**.
